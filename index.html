01 — About

From reporting on numbers to building the systems that produce them.

I started in data operations — dashboards, VBA macros, SQL checks. Somewhere along the way I realised the most valuable thing wasn't the report; it was making the data underneath trustworthy, and then automating everything that didn't need a human.

At Samsara I do exactly that for supply chain: a Claude-powered agent that runs our supply-history pipeline, a Forecast Accuracy Dashboard rebuilt as a Databricks App with natural-language querying, and the data models behind our Supply Chain Optimization App.

Outside work I keep learning in public — my AI Daily Study Notes project ships a fresh AI lesson to GitHub every morning.
